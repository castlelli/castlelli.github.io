iGEM USP 2026 worked on antibiotic resistance. The team's project, S(H)ARP, looks for hidden antibiotic pathways regulated in Streptomyces, and it competed in iGEM's Software and AI village.

I built the team wiki: a fully static Nuxt site deployed through GitLab CI, inside iGEM's compliance rules and its 11 MB size limit. I own the component library, the illustration and the scroll-driven landing.

On the software side I test, benchmark and review SHARP, the team's antibiotic hit finder.
