VivaPay replaced cash at VivaCTI, the annual fair at CTI-UNESP in Bauru. Visitors topped up a QR voucher at the cashier, and vendors at 40 booths charged it from their phones.

It ran for three days: 5,000+ people, 10,000 transactions, over R$10K processed. Balances are never stored. Each one is derived from an append-only log, so every real can be traced.

I led a team of seven student developers during our IT technician program. We built the API, the vendor app and the cashier desktop.
