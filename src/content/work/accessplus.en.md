AccessPlus helps students find scholarships, exchanges, olympiads and study programmes. It is free and made by public-school students.

I started as a full-stack developer and became Director of Innovation and Expansion, leading a team of eight. I automated the curation of opportunities in C# with the Gemini API, and the platform passed 5,000 opportunities.
