ROTIFER is a decade-old toolkit from the Protein Structural Evolution Lab at ICB-USP. It has about 200 command-line tools for comparative genomics: gene neighbourhoods, microsynteny and profile-based homology search.

I lead its engineering, as part of a FAPESP-funded project inside CEPID B3. The work is incremental. Interfaces change only through reviewed decisions, every merge goes through CI and review, and releases ship monthly.

So far: data-access modules for biological repositories, static analysis that surfaced critical bugs, public documentation and the community files a project needs to take outside contributions.

I presented the work as a poster at the CEPID B3 Annual Scientific Meeting 2026, where the abstract was published. A tool paper is in preparation, with me as a co-author.
