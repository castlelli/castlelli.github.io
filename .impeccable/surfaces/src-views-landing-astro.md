# Landing, One Pager, work pages

Scope: the whole site (landing `/`, One Pager `/about/`, project pages `/work/<slug>/`, PT mirrors). Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: research evaluators and founders/industry, equally. Primary action: open a project.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing is one continuous macro film the visitor drives by scrolling: a real-looking dev board, a dive into its screen, a protein sequence that becomes many, the sequences folding into a protein, the protein resolving into the Duffing attractor. It refuses the dev-portfolio hero over cards, the quiet journal page and the blueprint sheet.

OWN-WORLD: A dark studio lit like a product film: Coomassie-night sweep (#0B1030), a Coomassie solder-mask board with gold copper and an emissive OLED, papel light and type, marca-texto yellow only on the way into a project. Depth of field, film grain, soft bloom. Source Serif 4 display over the film; IBM Plex for labels and data. After the film the page returns to papel for the work, with Coomassie plates.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor sees an object built with care and learns who this is; scrolling, they watch hardware turn into biology and biology into mathematics, reading the three questions and the sequence-to-device captions over the beats, then open a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL: the board three-quarter view in studio light, OLED showing a terminal prompt, cursor tilts the camera. Over it, bottom-left: the name at display size, one line, the two projects as a contents list with yellow Open chips. A small scroll cue. Static poster frame of the same scene without WebGL.
