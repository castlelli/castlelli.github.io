# Landing, One Pager, work pages

Scope: the whole site's visual world (landing `/`, One Pager `/about/`, project pages `/work/<slug>/`, PT mirrors). Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: research evaluators and founders/industry, equally. Primary action: open a project.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the first page of a landmark technical paper, with the person as its subject and the projects as its contents. It refuses the dev-portfolio hero over a card grid, and refuses the old site's drawing-everywhere chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Journal paper (papel) ground, tinta ink, Source Serif 4 set like a 1950s journal: large title, italic byline and abstract, numbered section heads, figure captions "Fig. n —". Coomassie appears only as full-bleed figure plates and two plate sections, with papel line work. One hairline under the running head. Marca-texto yellow marks only the way into a project. Three type sizes per page.

STORY: In seconds the visitor learns who this is (engineer, builder, researcher across biology, open-source software and hardware), sees proof in beautiful figures and real numbers, and opens a project from the contents.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running head (V. Castelli · nav · Vol. 1 · 2026). Left: name at display size, italic byline (Poli-USP, LEEP ICB-USP), abstract, contents list of the projects with "Open →" (yellow on the first). Right: Fig. 1, a Coomassie plate with an animated schematic of the work (biology → software → hardware → health), caption below. Figures are the craft priority: they must be as beautiful as possible.

FORM: The Technical Journal (Bell System Technical Journal), position 4 of 7 on my grounded list. seed:d6499da7b48579cd
Raises: one focal figure per section (Saturday title card); blue only where something can be operated (bioluminescent wake); selected axis drawing bright, others ghosted (build instructions); yellow reserved for the way into a project (arcade cabinet); three type sizes per page (cutting bench).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
